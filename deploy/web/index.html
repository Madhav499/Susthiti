SUSTHITI is an AI-powered healthcare assistant providing early diabetes risk prediction, heart disease risk assessment, automated lab report extraction, real-time wearable tracking, and remote doctor-patient tele-care.

Core Platform Features

Designed for early disease detection, clinical tracking, and seamless doctor consultations.

🩺

Dual Machine Learning Models

Evaluates future diabetes risk (v4 API model) and 10-year cardiovascular disease risk (v3 Heart Model) using clinical parameters.

📄

AI Lab Report Extraction

Upload scanned medical lab reports or photo uploads (HbA1c, Lipid Profile, Glucose). OpenRouter AI parses key values automatically.

👨‍⚕️

Doctor-Patient Tele-Care

Doctors scan QR codes to request patient access, manage appointments, schedule surgeries, and prescribe medications remotely.

⌚

Wearable & Health Sync

Integrates with Android Health Connect to synchronize steps, sleep, and heart rate for continuous lifestyle trend analysis.

🔒

Secure Authentication & Resend Email

Role-based JWT authentication, rate limiting, and 8-digit numeric password recovery dispatched via transactional Resend email.

⚡

Production Cloud Infrastructure

Production backend hosted on Render cloud with PostgreSQL, automatic HTTPS, and Containerized FastAPI microservices.

How to Install on Android

Follow these quick steps to run the SUSTHITI app on your Android device.

1

Download the APK

Tap the Download Android App (APK) button above to download SUSTHITI.apk to your device.

2

Allow App Installation

Open your browser downloads or File Manager. Tap the downloaded APK file. If Android prompts a security warning, tap Settings and enable "Allow from this source" or "Install unknown apps" for your file manager/browser.

3

Complete Installation & Open

Tap Install. (If Google Play Protect displays a message regarding unlisted developer builds, tap "Install anyway"). Once installed, open SUSTHITI and create a patient account or test with demo credentials.
